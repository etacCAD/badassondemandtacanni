# Badass On Demand marketing plan

Badass On Demand is a live event on Fri, Nov 20, and every channel points at it now. Free Open Office Hours on Tue, Nov 17 is the warm-up that feeds it. After Nov 20, Office Hours runs every other week as the front door to the next event.

The goal is 10 new seats for Badass On Demand. Office Hours lands three days before the event, so everyone in that room gets a direct invite to Nov 20.

## Positioning

Badass On Demand is a live event for senior leaders, owners and founders whose growth now depends on how they lead. Its event page links to and from Mark's other sites and introduces Office Hours. The line: **Grows as fast as you lead.**

- **Who it's for:** owners and founders of roughly $2M–$50M companies with 10–250 people, and senior executives who lead leaders. A working definition for Mark to confirm.
- **The problem we name:** the leader is the bottleneck. Decisions, the story and ownership all route through one person.
- **What changes:** presence in high-stakes rooms, a story the team can repeat, decisions made at the right level, a team that owns outcomes, and a weekly growth rhythm. These are the five areas the assessment scores.
- **Proof we lead with:** Gallup's replacement cost of 0.5–2× salary and the 23% median profitability gap between top- and bottom-quartile engagement units. Client results from bigimpacthq.com stand in until leader testimonials exist.
- **The leader is the hero.** Mark appears as host and guide. Every page, post and email is about the reader's business.
- **Name decision:** keep "Badass On Demand" for owners, founders and events. For family offices and franchisors, lead with a subtitle ("Leadership performance for owners and senior leaders") and decide whether B2B one-sheets carry a quieter name.

## Audiences and buyers

One direct audience and four channel buyers. Each channel buyer brings a room of leaders at once.

| Audience | Who signs | What they buy | Why now | First offer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owners and founders (direct) | The owner | Their own growth past the bottleneck | Growth stalls once the first leaders are hired | A seat at Nov 20, or Office Hours first |
| Franchise systems | CEO, VP Operations or Training; franchise advisory council | Owner leadership across units | Unit performance, staff turnover and validation calls depend on the owner | Assessment pilot for 25–50 franchisees plus a convention session |
| Family offices | Principal, CIO, head of portfolio operations | Portfolio CEO leadership and next-gen readiness | Succession and key-person risk | Confidential diagnostic for 3–5 portfolio leaders |
| Incubators and accelerators | Program or managing director | A leadership outcome for the cohort | Founders stall after the raise, when the team doubles | Cohort workshop with the assessment at intake and graduation |
| Conference companies | Event producer, program director, meeting planner | MC and host, keynote, workshop | On-time agenda, sponsor value, rebooking | MC and host booking with a live assessment moment |

## Funnel

Channels (warm-list invites, peer referrals, LinkedIn content, the Kartra list, incubators, event producers, franchisors, family offices) send leaders either straight to the event page or through the Leadership Scale Assessment and Leadership Drag Calculator first. Leaders who aren't ready go to Office Hours. Every Office Hours attendee gets a follow-up within 24 hours with one offer that fits: the event, a VIP day, the community or a B2B program. Deeper offers are made in the room, never on the page.

## Open Office Hours engine

Every other Tuesday, 12:00–1:00 pm CT, free, capped at 20 leaders (placeholder schedule for Mark to confirm). Until Nov 20 it is the event's warm-up. After that, it is the only date on the event page.

**The message:** "You're changing the world. It's go time." Every invite names who belongs in the room, so the right leaders say "that's me."

- Building something that matters, and it has to scale.
- Carrying a payroll, and you take it personally.
- The reason it grew, and honest enough to admit you're why it's stuck.
- Done waiting to feel ready for the board, the raise or the stage.
- Willing to say the real problem out loud.
- Ready to make one hard move by next week.

The close: "Read that looking for reasons it isn't you? That's your answer." The sting targets hesitation, never the reader.

**The two-week cycle**

| Day | Action | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wed (day 1) | Recap post with one insight and the next date; recap email to registrants who missed it | Shiena |
| Thu–Mon (days 2–6) | 25 personal invites a day from the warm-list engine; Mark's LinkedIn post on one leadership gap | Shiena, Mark |
| Mon (day 6) | Email: take the assessment and bring your score | Shiena |
| Wed (day 8) | LinkedIn post with a calculator number or a hot-seat lesson | Mark |
| Fri (day 10) | Email to registrants: "What challenge will you bring?" | Shiena |
| Mon (day 13) | Reminder email and text; pick the hot seats | Shiena, Mark |
| Tue (day 14) | Session, with a reminder one hour before | Mark |

**Conversion rule:** every attendee hears back within 24 hours with their move, their score if they took the assessment, and one offer that fits.
