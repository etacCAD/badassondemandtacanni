- **Pattern:** a free or low-priced live session with a bonus you only get live, then the next step offered on the same call.
- **Pattern:** proof in dollars, not "confidence."
- **Open ground:** almost nobody owns "founder and CEO presence under pressure, on demand."
- **Price band:** paid live trainings run about $149–$695. For a fast fill, $97–$197; $297–$497 if attendees leave with a deliverable.

## Channel benchmarks

| Rank | Channel | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1:1 DMs and texts to warm contacts | Highest reply and conversion per hour |
| 2 | Past-attendee reactivation | The Big Impact Event list, by personal message |
| 3 | Peers as personal endorsers | Works only as named invites |
| 4 | Email countdown to the Kartra list | 7–9 sends; measure clicks, not opens |
| 5 | LinkedIn posts + LinkedIn Event | Free air cover |
| 6 | Reels and stories | Credibility, low direct yield in two weeks |
| 7 | $5–15/day retargeting | Only with a pixel and traffic |

- Warm LinkedIn DMs get replies 15–45% of the time vs 5–10% cold ([Valley](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/linkedin-reply-rate-benchmarks-2026), [Expandi](https://expandi.io/blog/state-of-li-outreach-h1-2025/)).
- Email clicks average 2.1% across industries, 1.28% for events ([MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/blog/email-marketing-benchmarks)).
- Registration pages convert 25–35% on average, 45–60% at best ([Learnybox](https://learnybox.com/blog/webinar-conversion-rate/)).
- Reminders at registration, a few days out and the day before lift attendance to 55–80% ([Sequenzy](https://www.sequenzy.com/email-marketing-for/workshop-facilitators)).
- What kills short promos: broadcast-only, waiting for the last 3 days, a vague outcome, fake scarcity, no reminders, mismatched details.
