## How the seats actually get filled

- **Named, personal invites beat broadcast posts.** Posts and graphics are air cover. Seats come from a person you trust saying "I thought of you for this."
- **Your own warm list carries most of the load.** Past Big Impact Event attendees, past clients, VIP and Big Stage alumni, and your LinkedIn connections. Peer referrals add to that; they can't replace it.
- **Run the play you already run for clients.** Affiliates, JVs and a 30-minute connection call with each partner, with Shiena executing and each referrer treated as a hand-picked affiliate.
- **The one-sentence test.** Anyone promoting it must be able to say who it's for, what changes, the date, the price and the link. Fill in the TBCs first.

### Referral list exercise (10 minutes)

Write one first name per prompt. Stop at 10. Don't judge fit yet.

1. Someone who just got promoted, hired a team or launched something.
2. Someone who presents, pitches or speaks and complains about it.
3. Someone who runs sales calls or demos and could close more.
4. Someone you've seen freeze, ramble or undersell in a meeting.
5. A founder who has to raise money, recruit or lead from the front.
6. A past client or colleague who's good but not visible enough.
7. Someone on your team you'd like to see lead more.
8. Someone in another group who can't explain what they do.
9. Someone you'd be proud to introduce to Mark.
10. Someone who keeps saying they need to "get out there."

Circle your top 5. Next to each, write one sentence on why it fits them. That sentence goes into the message.

### Incentive menu (Mark picks one)

- **Bring a guest:** free or half-price seat for the person you invite. Best fit for a peer circle.
- **Comp seat:** 2 confirmed registrations earns the referrer a seat.
- **Seat for a cause:** $X per seat to a charity the group picks.
- **Group goal:** 10 seats and Mark hosts the next dinner.
- **Revenue share:** only if someone asks. Reads as transactional with peers.

### What to avoid

Identical posts across referrers (reads as a campaign), claims the referrer can't back from personal knowledge, hype and ALL CAPS, links in the body of LinkedIn posts, fake scarcity.

## Tracking (under a day to set up)

- Add each referrer as a Kartra affiliate with $0 or non-cash commission, so each gets a link and you get a report. Confirm Kartra allows $0.
- Fallback: a UTM link per referrer, e.g. `?utm_source=referral&utm_content=firstname`.
- A required "Who invited you?" field on both forms. Most referrals arrive by text with no click.
- Shiena reconciles once a day. When the link and the answer disagree, the answer wins.
